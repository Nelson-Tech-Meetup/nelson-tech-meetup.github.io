## Source Cooperative

Anthony Lukach shares his recent work re-architecting [Source Cooperative](https://source.coop), a platform for storing and distributing 5+ petabytes of scientific datasets. He'll discuss the case for flat-file architectures, how cloud-native geospatial formats unlock scalable data distribution, and why Cloudflare Workers became a key part of the stack.
