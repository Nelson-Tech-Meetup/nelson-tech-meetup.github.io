Anthony Lukach shares his recent work re-architecting [Source Cooperative](https://source.coop), an open source platform operated by the non-profit [Radiant Earth](https://radiant.earth/) that stores and distributes 5+ petabytes of scientific datasets across various cloud providers. He'll discuss how cloud-optimized file formats are replacing APIs, the overall design of the Source Cooperative platform, and why Cloudflare Workers became a key part of the stack.
